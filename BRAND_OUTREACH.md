# PAWS — First Brand Outreach (the pilot email)

**Target:** a small DTC pet brand (treats, supplements, or fresh food) that
can decide fast — NOT a giant. The pilot is $500–2,500 flat for a 30-day
branded mission. This email is the ask.

---

## Email 1 — the pilot ask (30–60 seconds to read)

**Subject:** A pet-app pilot: we pay for your customer's meals — you get
consumption data no panel has

Hi [Name],

I run **PAWS**, a pet-app where families log their dogs' meals, vaccines,
and purchases. Here's what we built (it's live):

- **The Feeding Log** — families tap "Fed" 2× a day, recording the exact
  brand and grams (the app even tells them the right portion for their
  dog's weight — most families feed by guesswork).
- **The Share-of-Stomach panel** — what's actually being fed, when, and
  whether the dog switched brands. This is consumption data that
  **no retail-transaction panel can show** — a bag bought at the store
  doesn't mean it's being fed.
- **Brand-funded missions** — the mechanism is built and live: "Feed
  [Your Brand] for 7 days → 200 pts" with real-time progress.

**The pilot:** $1,000 for 30 days. We run a "Feed [Brand] for 7 days"
mission to our active pet parents, and you get:
1. **Share-of-stomach before/after** — how many households switched to
   you, and whether they stayed (the loyalty experiment).
2. **The meal log itself** — real consumption events, not surveys.
3. **A live demo** — you watch your mission run in the app.

We're small, so you're testing with early-adopter pet parents who are
actually engaged, not a phone-book sample.

Worth a 15-minute call this week? I'll bring the live demo.

— [Your name], PAWS

## Why this target list (order of approach)

1. **DTC fresh/supplement brands** (Farmer's Dog, Ollie, Zesty Paws,
   local freeze-dried): subscription brands live or die on switching
   data — they'd pay for "who switched to us and stayed."
2. **A local/regional pet store** (independents): they compete with
   Chewy/PetSmart on relationships; a community rewards pilot is their
   kind of marketing, and they can decide in days.
3. **Treat/dental brands** (Greenies-adjacent, smaller): a "Log 5 meals"
   mission is cheap for them and puts their product in the feed.

## The numbers we can show (the demo speaks)

- Live app: onboarding → feeding log → missions → coupons → passport
- The panel: purchases + **share of stomach** + health + recall data
- GS1 DataBar coupons: minted, single-use, shop-validated
- Real engagement mechanics: streaks, notifications, recall alerts

## The honest constraints (so the call isn't wasted)

- Panel is small (demo scale, ~10 pets). The pilot is a **proof of
  mechanism**, not a census. That's exactly why it's $1,000, not $50k.
- The mission engine, feeding log, and share-of-stomach panel are real
  and working; the audience is the thing that grows.
- We're honest about what's demo vs production — the demo has to be.
